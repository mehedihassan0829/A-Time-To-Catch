Pixilart files for the GameJam 2026 game (Park Street, Bristol)

Open any .pixil file at pixilart.com: Draw -> File -> Open, then pick the file.
Every drawing is at its true pixel size (the game scales each pixel up 4x).
Files with more than one frame are animations (walk cycles, the waterfall, the doner spit...).

tiles/       ground tiles, 16x16 (grass, paving, road, crossing, stairs, water, shop and club floors)
props/       trees, lamps, benches, railings, statues, cars, the bus, traffic lights (each state),
             City Hall's fountain, the notebook, watch and lighter, the dog
interiors/   shop furniture and wall pieces, the doner spit, coffee steam
characters/  the detective's walk cycles (one file per direction) and everyone else in the game
  story/           the story characters from story.py (Dez, Maggie, Fitz, Ray)
  npc_jobs/        people at work: barber cutting, doner man carving, barista, gym, bookseller...
  npc_pedestrians/ a sample of the people walking round Park Street
  npc_night/       who comes out after dark: people in hoods, students on a night out
buildings/   landmarks and every walk-in shop on Park Street
signs/       every shop's brand sign
map/         the whole Park Street map (and in four sections), and Frogmore Street under the bridge

Building outlines are based on OpenStreetMap: Map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors.

Drawings added for the story:

  story/                  the crime scene, the rooms' furniture (offices, the arcade store room, the hostel, the flat),
